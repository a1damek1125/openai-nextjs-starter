## Honest summary

**What is real:** the complete decision pipeline — gate → routing → consent → rate
limit → compose → approval → send-with-fallback → delivery tracking → audit — runs as
tested, deterministic code, with the safety-critical behaviors (opt-out blocks,
business-hours deferral, no-price composer, human approval with recorded edits,
tamper-evident audit chain, hashed upload tokens) proven by 25 passing tests including
three full E2E scenarios.

**What is not:** everything that makes it a *service*. There is no HTTP server, no
database, no real provider, no durable scheduler, no webhook receiver, no RBAC, no RLS.
All three infrastructure adapters are mocks — honest, Protocol-shaped mocks, but mocks.
Restart today and you lose the approval queue, rate-limit history, scheduled sends, and
the audit chain.

**The shape of the remaining work is favorable:** the engine makes no decisions in the
adapters and the adapters make no decisions in the engine, so productionizing is
persistence + transport + auth around a tested core, not a rewrite. The P0 column is
the pilot blocker list: persistence with RLS, durable Temporal, signed webhooks, RBAC
on approvals, idempotency, and the DB-backed audit chain. Until those land, this is a
verified specification-in-code — nothing more, and honestly, nothing less.
